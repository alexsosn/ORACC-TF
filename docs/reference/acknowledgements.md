# Acknowledgements

The converted Assyrian inscription texts, underlying editions,
transliterations, signs, lemmas, translations and catalogue scholarship
were contributed by the **ORACC** research projects, notably **RIAO**
and **RINAP**, and their individual scholarly editors and
annotators. **ORACC-TF** provides the Text-Fabric conversion,
data modelling, source-mapping checks, app packaging and documentation;
it does not claim authorship of those underlying editions.

Editorial credit is **text-specific**. For the actual
`riao/ria1:Q001801` source, the RIAO catalogue references
**A. Kirk Grayson** as the basis of the printed edition and credits
**Jamie Novotny** for adaptation and **Nathan Morello** for
lemmatization/update. These are facts about this *particular*
source record, not a complete editors-of-RIAO list or a claim
that all RINAP texts share those contributors.

For any cited inscription, inspect the ORACC catalogue and
the individual edition's publication/credits fields to identify
the correct editors, source edition and translation.
The source-specific bibliography and editorial responsibility
remain with the original publishing projects. See
[References](references.md) and the [corpus scope](scope.md).

Text-Fabric is developed separately by the Text-Fabric project,
which supplies the graph format, `T.text`/feature APIs and
the browser. The ORACC-TF software contributors are credited
for the converter and the generated representation only.

Reuse rights must be kept as specific as the sources. The
[**citation and licence guidance**](citation.md) documents the
CC0 metadata versus CC BY-SA annotated-edition tension and
separate translation attribution, while
[Known issues](known-issues.md) identifies measured gaps.
The MIT software licence is not an additional grant of rights
over the original inscription editions or translations.
